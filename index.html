FAQ

Questions worth asking

What's the difference between O Level and IGCSE Physics?
The physics overlaps heavily. The differences are mainly in paper structure and assessment — 0625 has more paper variants including separate practical options. Which one you sit is decided by your school's entry, not by you.
How does the student portal work?
You get a student ID at enrollment. Signing in opens exactly the course you registered for, with lectures grouped by module. Recorded lectures play in place; live sessions open a Zoom link.
Do you teach students outside Pakistan?
Yes. Live class times are shown in your local timezone once you enrol, and card payment is available for international students.
Can I join mid-session?
Usually yes. Recorded modules cover what you've missed, and a short diagnostic works out what needs catching up first.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — a free demo lesson before you commit. No payment details needed.
What if I'm retaking?
A retake starts with a past paper diagnostic, so time goes to the topics where marks were actually lost rather than the whole syllabus again.
Do you help with the practical papers?
Yes. Practical technique is taught explicitly, and A2 Paper 5 gets its own slot because it rewards method more than knowledge.
